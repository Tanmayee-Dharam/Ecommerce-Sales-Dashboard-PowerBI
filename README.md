# Ecommerce-Sales-Dashboard-PowerBI
Created an Interactive Dashboard of an E-commerce Store

# Project Objective:
The primary objective of this project was to assist the store owner in gaining insights into their online sales data. By creating an interactive and visually appealing dashboard, we aimed to provide the owner with a tool to make data-driven decisions and enhance their online sales strategy.

# Data Source:
The sales data is stored in CSV format, split across two files. The following steps were followed in the project:
1. Extract the Data: The sales data was extracted from the provided CSV files.
2. Transform the Data: Power BI's Power Query editor was utilized to transform and clean the raw data, making it suitable for analysis.
3. Load the Data in Power BI: The transformed data was loaded into Power BI for visualization and analysis.

# Questions (KPIs) :
1. Compare the sum of the amounts with different states.
2. Which person spent the maximum amount of money to buy products?
3. Which category of product do customers order the most (Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture)?
4. What kind of payment mode do customers use the most while purchasing products?
5. Which months of the year does this Store generate the maximum profit and minimum profit?
6. Which sub-category of the product do customers order the most and contribute to gaining maximum profits for the Store?

# Process:
1. Created an interactive dashboard to track and analyze online sales data.
2. Then, used complex parameters to drill down in worksheets and customization using filters and slicers.
3. Created connections, joined new tables, and performed calculations to manipulate data and enable user-driven parameters for visualizations.
4. Later, used different types of customized visualization (bar chart, pie chart, donut chart, clustered bar chart, scatter chart, line chart, area chart, map, slicers, etc).

# Project Insight:
1. The majority of customers have spent the highest amounts on products in categories like Clothing (63%), Electronics (21%), and Furniture (17%), including items such as printers, bookcases, and sarees.
2. The store sees peak profits during January to April and again in December.
3. Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Uttar Pradesh are the leading states in terms of purchase volume.
4. Harivansh, Madhav, and Madan Mohan are the top three customers contributing the most to sales.
5. In terms of payment preferences, most customers opt for Cash on Delivery (44%), followed by UPI (21%).

# Final Conclusion:
The customers living in Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Uttar Pradesh purchase the most of the products such as Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture. To improve sales of the Madhav E-Commerce Store, the owner must show maximum ads, sale offers/coupons in the months from January to April and December.
